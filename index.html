Stage 1 Traffic

Metric: VR (Visit Rate) = page visits ÷ people reached, across every channel

VR = PV ÷ (PE + CIS + LRS + AI + PI)
PV = PE × VRCE + CIS × VRCI + LRS × VRL + AI × VRA + PI × VRP
VRCE = EDR × EOR × ELCR × PLR
VRCI = IDR × ICR × PLR
VRL = LAR × LMR × LCR × PLR
VRA = ACTR × APVR
VRP = PLCR × PLR
  • VRVisit Rate: page visits ÷ people reached, across every channel
  • PVPage Visits
  • PEPeople Emailed
  • CISCalendar Invites Sent
  • LRSLinkedIn Requests Sent: connection requests
  • AIAd Impressions
  • PIPost Impressions
  • VRCEVisit Rate, Cold Email
  • VRCIVisit Rate, Calendar Invites
  • VRLVisit Rate, LinkedIn
  • VRAVisit Rate, Ads
  • VRPVisit Rate, Posts
  • EDREmail Delivery Rate: emails delivered ÷ emails sent
  • EOREmail Open Rate: people who opened ÷ people who received it
  • ELCREmail Link Click Rate: people who clicked ÷ people who opened
  • PLRPage Load Rate: page visits ÷ link clicks (set by page speed)
  • IDRInvite Delivery Rate: invites delivered ÷ invites sent
  • ICRInvite Click Rate: clicks on the invite link ÷ invites delivered
  • LARLinkedIn Acceptance Rate: requests accepted ÷ requests sent
  • LMRLinkedIn Message Rate: people messaged ÷ people who accepted
  • LCRLinkedIn Click Rate: link clicks ÷ people messaged
  • ACTRAd Click-Through Rate: ad clicks ÷ impressions
  • APVRAd Page View Rate: page views ÷ ad clicks
  • PLCRPost Link Click Rate: link clicks ÷ post impressions
Stage 2 Landing and Sign-Up

Metric: PAR (Page-to-Attendee Rate) = attendees who signed up on the page ÷ page visits

PAR = RR × PSSR
RR = PSR × FVR × FSR × FCR
PSSR = NASR × (1 − TAR) + ASR × TAR
R = PV × RR + AR
AR = CSU + ESU + LSU + ISU
CSU = CIS × IDR × (YR + MR)
ESU = PE × EDR × ERR × EIS
LSU = LRS × LAR × LMR × LRR × LIS
ISU = PI × PER × IMR × IRR
  • PARPage-to-Attendee Rate: attendees who signed up on the page ÷ page visits
  • RRRegistration Rate: sign-ups ÷ page visits
  • PSSRPage Sign-Up Show Rate: page sign-ups who attended ÷ page sign-ups
  • PSRPage Stay Rate: visits that stayed 10 seconds or more ÷ page visits (100% minus bounce rate)
  • FVRForm View Rate: people who scrolled to the form ÷ people who stayed
  • FSRForm Start Rate: people who started the form ÷ people who saw it
  • FCRForm Completion Rate: people who submitted ÷ people who started
  • NASRNo-Action Show Rate: show-up rate of page sign-ups who skipped the thank you page action
  • TARThank You Action Rate: people who did the action ÷ people who saw the thank you page
  • ASRAction Show Rate: show-up rate of page sign-ups who did the thank you page action (add to calendar, join WhatsApp)
  • RRegistrants: page sign-ups plus auto-registrants
  • PVPage Visits
  • ARAuto-Registrants: people registered without the form (calendar Yes or Maybe, interested replies, inbound messages)
  • CSUCalendar Sign-Ups: registered by a Yes or Maybe on the calendar invite
  • ESUEmail Sign-Ups: registered by an interested reply to cold email
  • LSULinkedIn Sign-Ups: registered by an interested reply on LinkedIn
  • ISUInbound Sign-Ups: registered from inbound messages after posts
  • CISCalendar Invites Sent
  • IDRInvite Delivery Rate: invites delivered ÷ invites sent
  • YRYes Rate: Yes ÷ invites delivered
  • MRMaybe Rate: Maybe ÷ invites delivered
  • PEPeople Emailed
  • EDREmail Delivery Rate: emails delivered ÷ emails sent
  • ERREmail Reply Rate: people who replied ÷ people who received the email
  • EISEmail Interested Share: interested replies ÷ all email replies
  • LRSLinkedIn Requests Sent: connection requests
  • LARLinkedIn Acceptance Rate: requests accepted ÷ requests sent
  • LMRLinkedIn Message Rate: people messaged ÷ people who accepted
  • LRRLinkedIn Reply Rate: people who replied ÷ people messaged
  • LISLinkedIn Interested Share: interested replies ÷ all LinkedIn replies
  • PIPost Impressions
  • PERPost Engagement Rate: likes, comments and shares ÷ post impressions
  • IMRInbound Message Rate: inbound messages ÷ engagements
  • IRRInbound Registration Rate: inbound people registered ÷ inbound messages
Stage 3 Reminders and Nurture

Metric: SUR (Show-Up Rate) = attendees ÷ registrants

SUR = DJR + EJ + WJ + SJ + CJ
A = R × SUR
EJ = REDR × REOR × EJCR
WJ = WOR × WDR × WRR × WJCR
SJ = SRR × SDR × SJCR
CJ = PS × CAR × CCR × CACR
  • SURShow-Up Rate: attendees ÷ registrants
  • DJRDirect Join Rate: people who joined without clicking any reminder ÷ registrants
  • EJEmail Joins: people whose first join came from an email link ÷ registrants
  • WJWhatsApp Joins: people whose first join came from a WhatsApp link ÷ registrants
  • SJSMS Joins: people whose first join came from a text link ÷ registrants
  • CJCall Joins: people who joined after confirming on the call ÷ registrants
  • AAttendees
  • RRegistrants: page sign-ups plus auto-registrants
  • REDRReminder Email Delivery Rate: delivered ÷ sent
  • REORReminder Email Open Rate: people who opened a reminder ÷ people who got one
  • EJCREmail Join Click Rate: people who joined from an email link ÷ people who opened
  • WORWhatsApp Opt-In Rate: people who opted in ÷ registrants
  • WDRWhatsApp Delivery Rate: delivered ÷ sent
  • WRRWhatsApp Read Rate: read ÷ delivered
  • WJCRWhatsApp Join Click Rate: people who joined from a WhatsApp link ÷ people who read
  • SRRSMS Reach Rate: registrants with a phone and text consent ÷ registrants
  • SDRSMS Delivery Rate: delivered ÷ sent
  • SJCRSMS Join Click Rate: people who joined from a text link ÷ texts delivered
  • PSPhone Share: registrants the AI caller can ring ÷ registrants
  • CARCall Answer Rate: calls answered by a person ÷ people called
  • CCRCall Confirm Rate: people who said they will come ÷ calls answered
  • CACRCame After Confirming Rate: people who joined ÷ people who confirmed
Stage 4 Booking the Call

Metric: BR (Booking Rate) = calls booked ÷ attendees

BR = LBR + (1 − LBR) × FUCR × BCR + NSA × RWR × RCR × BCR
LBR = OSR × OCTR × BCR
OCTR = ES × EOCR + (1 − ES) × QOCR
NSA = (R − A) ÷ A
CB = A × BR
  • BRBooking Rate: calls booked ÷ attendees
  • LBRLive Booking Rate: calls booked during the event ÷ attendees
  • FUCRFollow-Up Click Rate: booking-link clicks from follow-up messages ÷ people who had not booked
  • BCRBooking Completion Rate: calls booked ÷ offer clicks
  • NSANo-Shows per Attendee: (registrants − attendees) ÷ attendees
  • RWRReplay Watch Rate: replay viewers ÷ no-shows
  • RCRReplay Click Rate: offer clicks from the replay ÷ replay viewers
  • OSROffer Stay Rate: people present at the offer minute ÷ attendees
  • OCTROffer Click-Through Rate: offer clicks ÷ people at the offer
  • ESEngaged Share: people who answered a poll, asked a question or chatted ÷ people at the offer
  • EOCREngaged Offer Click Rate: offer clicks ÷ engaged people at the offer
  • QOCRQuiet Offer Click Rate: offer clicks ÷ quiet people at the offer
  • RRegistrants: page sign-ups plus auto-registrants
  • AAttendees
  • CBCalls Booked
Stage 5 The Sales Pipeline

Metric: CR (Close Rate) = onboarded customers ÷ calls booked

CR = CHR × QR × PR × WR × PCR × OCR
CHR = CSR + (1 − CSR) × RBR × CSR
CSR = USR + ECS + WCS + SCS + CCS
ECS = CRDR × CROR × ESCR
WCS = BWR × WDR × WRR × WSCR
SCS = BSR × SDR × SSCR
CCS = BPS × RCAR × CSCR
RBR = NF1R + NF2R + NF3R + NF4R + NF5R
WR = WWC + DF1R + DF2R + DF3R
OC = CB × CR

  • PCRPayment Completion Rate: payments completed ÷ proposals accepted
  • OCROnboarding Completion Rate: customers onboarded ÷ customers who paid
  • CSRCall Show Rate: calls held the first time ÷ calls booked
  • RBRRebook Rate: no-shows who rebooked ÷ no-shows (the five follow-ups added)
  • USRUnprompted Show Rate: showed with no reminder click ÷ calls booked
  • ECSEmail Confirmed Shows: showed after confirming from the email reminder ÷ calls booked
  • WCSWhatsApp Confirmed Shows: showed after confirming from the WhatsApp reminder ÷ calls booked
  • SCSSMS Confirmed Shows: showed after confirming from the SMS reminder ÷ calls booked
  • CCSCall Confirmed Shows: showed after confirming on the AI call ÷ calls booked
  • CRDRCall Reminder Delivery Rate: delivered ÷ sent
  • CRORCall Reminder Open Rate: opened ÷ delivered
  • ESCREmail Show Confirm Rate: confirmed and showed ÷ people who opened
  • BWRBooked WhatsApp Reach: booked contacts with WhatsApp ÷ calls booked
  • WDRWhatsApp Delivery Rate: delivered ÷ sent
  • WRRWhatsApp Read Rate: read ÷ delivered
  • WSCRWhatsApp Show Confirm Rate: confirmed and showed ÷ read
  • BSRBooked SMS Reach: booked contacts with a phone and consent ÷ calls booked
  • SDRSMS Delivery Rate: delivered ÷ sent
  • SSCRSMS Show Confirm Rate: confirmed and showed ÷ delivered
  • BPSBooked Phone Share: booked contacts the AI caller can ring ÷ calls booked
  • RCARReminder Call Answer Rate: answered ÷ called
  • CSCRCall Show Confirm Rate: confirmed and showed ÷ answered
  • NF1RNo-Show Follow-Up 1 Rate: rebooked after no-show follow-up 1 (sent right away) ÷ no-shows
  • NF2RNo-Show Follow-Up 2 Rate: rebooked after follow-up 2 (3 days later) ÷ no-shows

  • DF2RDeal Follow-Up 2 Rate: accepted after deal follow-up 2 (day 7) ÷ proposals sent

  • CBCalls Booked
Stage 6 The Next 52 Weeks

Metric: YRR (Yearly Rebooking Rate) = calls booked from the 52-week messages ÷ people on the list

YRR = WEB + ANB + NWB + NSB + CICB
WEB = WER × WES × WEDR × WEOR × WECR × CBR
ANB = ANR × ANS × ANDR × ANOR × ANCR × CBR
NWB = NWR × NWS × NWDR × NWRR × NWCR × CBR
NSB = NSR × NSS × NSDR × NSCR × CBR
CICB = CICR × CIC × CIAR × CIBR
LS = R − OC
YCB = LS × YRR

  • WESWeekly Email Sends: weekly email sends per person per year
  • WEDRWeekly Email Delivery Rate: delivered ÷ sent
  • WEORWeekly Email Open Rate: opened ÷ delivered

  • CBRClick-to-Booking Rate: calls booked ÷ clicks from 52-week messages
  • ANRAI Newsletter Reach: people who can get AI newsletter ÷ people on the list
  • ANSAI Newsletter Sends: AI newsletter sends per person per year
