Every Equation, Explained

91 short equations take you from the first email to the last renewal, for a webinar, a reverse squeeze page and a workshop series. Each one uses only four signs: plus, minus, times and divided by. Every part is explained in plain words, with where to find the number and what to do when it is weak.

How to Read Every Equation

  1. Colours tell you what a number is.
    Bluea count of people or thingsPurplea rate: out of every 100GreenmoneyAmbera plain numberDarkthe answer
  2. Every rate is a count divided by the count before it. "Open Rate" is the people who opened divided by the people who received the email. That is why every equation is exactly true for your own numbers.
  3. Times keeps a share; plus adds groups together. When you multiply by a rate you keep the part who took the step. When you add, each person was counted in only one group, so nobody is counted twice.
  4. The weakest rate is the one to fix. Compare each rate with its typical range. The one furthest below is costing you the most.

Delivered Rate
A rate: out of every 100
What it means
Out of every 100 people you emailed, how many actually received it (it did not bounce).

People who received it ÷ People emailed. It is also 100% minus your bounce rate.

Check every address with a verifier before you send.

A rate: out of every 100
What it means
Out of every 100 people who received the email, how many opened it.
How to work it out
People who opened ÷ People who received it.

Out of every 100 people who opened, how many clicked the link to your page.

2% to 8% · Measure yours on your first run

  1. Every stage and every channel has its own short equation, using only plus, minus, times and divided by.

  5. The 52 weeks keep everyone who did not buy in touch, and every new booking keeps its source.
