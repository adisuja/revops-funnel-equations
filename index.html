The RevOps Funnel Equations

Every rate is one count divided by the count before it. Each variable is defined where it first appears.

The Whole Funnel in One Line

1Webinar and Workshop Series

Paying Customers = Total Registrants × Show-Up Rate × Booked per Attendee × Paid per Booked Call

  • Total Registrants: from #19
  • Show-Up Rate: from #34
  • Booked per Attendee: Booked calls ÷ Attendees.
  • Paid per Booked Call: Paying customers ÷ Booked calls.
2Reverse Squeeze Page

Paying Customers = Watched More Than Half × Booked per Half-Watcher × Paid per Booked Call

  • Watched More Than Half: from #36
  • Booked per Half-Watcher: Booked calls ÷ People who watched more than half.

Stage 1: Traffic

3Cold Email: Page Visits

Page Visits From Email = People Emailed × Delivered Rate × Open Rate × Click Rate × Page Load Rate

  • People Emailed: How many different people your cold email campaign reached.
  • Delivered Rate: People who received it ÷ People emailed.
  • Open Rate: People who opened ÷ People who received it.
  • Click Rate: People who clicked ÷ People who opened.
  • Page Load Rate: Page visits ÷ Link clicks.
4Cold Email: Sign-Ups by Reply

Signed Up by Email Reply = People Emailed × Delivered Rate × Reply Rate × Yes Rate

  • Reply Rate: People who replied ÷ People who received it.
  • Yes Rate: Replies saying yes ÷ All replies.
5Calendar Invites: Sign-Ups by Yes or Maybe

Signed Up by Calendar Yes or Maybe = Calendar Invites Sent × Invite Delivered Rate × (RSVP Yes Rate + RSVP Maybe Rate)

  • Calendar Invites Sent: How many calendar invites for your event you sent.
  • Invite Delivered Rate: Invites that arrived ÷ Invites sent.
  • RSVP Yes Rate: People who clicked Yes ÷ Invites that arrived.
  • RSVP Maybe Rate: People who clicked Maybe ÷ Invites that arrived.
6Calendar Invites: Page Visits

Page Visits From Invites = Calendar Invites Sent × Invite Delivered Rate × Invite Link Click Rate × Page Load Rate

  • Invite Link Click Rate: Link clicks from invites ÷ Invites that arrived.
7LinkedIn: Page Visits

Page Visits From LinkedIn = LinkedIn Requests Sent × Acceptance Rate × Message Reach × LinkedIn Link Click Rate × Page Load Rate

  • LinkedIn Requests Sent: How many connection requests you sent on LinkedIn.
  • Acceptance Rate: People who accepted ÷ Requests sent.
  • Message Reach: People who got the message ÷ People who accepted.
  • LinkedIn Link Click Rate: People who clicked ÷ People messaged.
8LinkedIn: Sign-Ups by Reply

Signed Up by LinkedIn Reply = LinkedIn Requests Sent × Acceptance Rate × Message Reach × LinkedIn Reply Rate × LinkedIn Yes Rate

  • LinkedIn Reply Rate: People who replied ÷ People messaged.
  • LinkedIn Yes Rate: Replies saying yes ÷ All replies.
9Ads: Cost per Click

Cost per Click = Cost per 1,000 Views ÷ 1,000 ÷ Ad Click Rate

  • Cost per 1,000 Views: What the platform charges each time your ad is shown 1,000 times.
  • Ad Click Rate: Link clicks ÷ Times shown.
10Ads: Page Visits

Page Visits From Ads = Ad Spend ÷ Cost per Click × Ad Page View Rate

  • Ad Spend: How much money you spent on ads that send people to your page.
  • Cost per Click: from #9
  • Ad Page View Rate: Page views from ads ÷ Ad clicks.
11Ads: Lead Form Sign-Ups

Lead Form Sign-Ups = Lead Form Ad Spend ÷ Cost per Lead Form Sign-Up

  • Lead Form Ad Spend: Money spent on ads where people sign up inside the ad itself (no page visit).
  • Cost per Lead Form Sign-Up: What one sign-up costs on a lead form ad.
12Posts: Page Visits

Page Visits From Posts = Post Views × Post Link Click Rate × Page Load Rate

  • Post Views: How many times your posts were seen.
  • Post Link Click Rate: Link clicks from posts ÷ Post views.
13Posts: Inbound Messages

Inbound Messages = Post Views × Engagement Rate × Message Rate

  • Engagement Rate: (Likes + Comments + Shares) ÷ Post views.
  • Message Rate: Messages and 'I want in' comments ÷ People who engaged.
14Posts: Sign-Ups From Messages

Signed Up From Messages = Inbound Messages × Inbound Registered Rate

  • Inbound Messages: from #13
  • Inbound Registered Rate: Inbound people registered ÷ Inbound messages.
15All Channels: Total Page Visits

Total Page Visits = Page Visits From Email + Page Visits From Invites + Page Visits From LinkedIn + Page Visits From Ads + Page Visits From Posts

  • Page Visits From Email: from #3
  • Page Visits From Invites: from #6
  • Page Visits From LinkedIn: from #7
  • Page Visits From Ads: from #10
  • Page Visits From Posts: from #12
16All Channels: Signed Up Without the Form

Signed Up Without the Form = Signed Up by Email Reply + Signed Up by Calendar Yes or Maybe + Signed Up by LinkedIn Reply + Lead Form Sign-Ups + Signed Up From Messages

  • Signed Up by Email Reply: from #4
  • Signed Up by Calendar Yes or Maybe: from #5
  • Signed Up by LinkedIn Reply: from #8
  • Lead Form Sign-Ups: from #11
  • Signed Up From Messages: from #14

Stage 2: Landing and Sign-Up

17The Sign-Up Rate, Step by Step

Sign-Up Rate = Stay Rate × Form Seen Rate × Form Start Rate × Form Finish Rate

  • Stay Rate: Visits that stayed 10 seconds or more ÷ All visits.
  • Form Seen Rate: People who saw the form ÷ People who stayed.
  • Form Start Rate: People who started the form ÷ People who saw it.
  • Form Finish Rate: People who submitted ÷ People who started.

21Thank You Page 2Webinar · Workshop Series

Video Plays = Total Page Visits × Stay Rate × Video Play Rate

  • Video Play Rate: People who played the video ÷ People who stayed.
23The Video Page: Watched More Than HalfReverse Squeeze Page

Watched Half (First Visit) = Video Plays × Watched-Half Rate

  • Video Plays: from #22

People You Can Still Remind = Left Before Halfway × Part 2 Sign-Up Rate (Left Early) + Signed Up Without the Form

  • Left Before Halfway: from #24
  • Part 2 Sign-Up Rate (Left Early): Part 2 sign-ups from people who left early ÷ People who left early.
27Everyone You Can RemindReverse Squeeze Page

Everyone You Can Remind = Part 2 Sign-Ups (Watched Half) + People You Can Still Remind

  • Part 2 Sign-Ups (Watched Half): from #25
  • People You Can Still Remind: from #26
