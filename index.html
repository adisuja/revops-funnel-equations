The RevOps Funnel Equations

One metric per stage, per funnel. Each stage's equation shows what that metric is made of, channel by channel. Every rate is one count divided by another.

Whole Funnel
C = (V × RR + AR) × SU × BR × CL
  • C onboarded customers
  • V page visits from every channel
  • RR registration rate: sign-ups ÷ page visits
  • AR auto-registrants: people registered without the form (calendar Yes or Maybe, interested replies, inbound messages)
  • SU Show-Up Rate: attendees ÷ registrants
  • BR Booking Rate: calls booked ÷ attendees
  • CL Close Rate: onboarded customers ÷ calls booked
Stage 1 Traffic

Metric: VR (Visit Rate) = page visits ÷ people reached, across every channel

VR = V ÷ (Ne + Nc + Nl + Na + No)
V = Ne × VRe + Nc × VRc + Nl × VRl + Na × VRa + No × VRo
VRe = DRe × ORe × CRe × LR
VRc = DRc × CLc × LR
VRl = ACl × MSl × CRl × LR
VRa = CTa × LVa
VRo = CTo × LR
  • VR Visit Rate: page visits ÷ people reached, across every channel
  • V page visits from every channel
  • Ne people emailed
  • Nc calendar invites sent
  • Nl LinkedIn connection requests sent
  • Na ad impressions
  • No post impressions
  • VRe visit rate of cold email
  • VRc visit rate of calendar invites
  • VRl visit rate of LinkedIn outreach
  • VRa visit rate of ads
  • VRo visit rate of posts
  • DRe email delivered rate: emails delivered ÷ emails sent
  • ORe email open rate: people who opened ÷ people who received it
  • CRe email click rate: people who clicked ÷ people who opened
  • LR page load rate: page visits ÷ link clicks (set by page speed)
  • DRc invite delivered rate: invites delivered ÷ invites sent
  • CLc invite link click rate: clicks on the invite link ÷ invites delivered
  • ACl acceptance rate: requests accepted ÷ requests sent
  • MSl message reach: people messaged ÷ people who accepted
  • CRl LinkedIn click rate: link clicks ÷ people messaged
  • CTa ad click-through rate: ad clicks ÷ impressions
  • LVa ad page view rate: page views ÷ ad clicks
  • CTo post click-through rate: link clicks ÷ post impressions
Stage 2 Landing and Sign-Up

Metric: PA (Page-to-Attendee Rate) = attendees who signed up on the page ÷ page visits

PA = RR × SUf
RR = ST × FS × FT × FC
SUf = SU0 × (1 − T1) + SU1 × T1
R = V × RR + AR
AR = ARc + ARe + ARl + ARo
ARc = Nc × DRc × (YS + MY)
ARe = Ne × DRe × RPe × YE
ARl = Nl × ACl × MSl × RPl × YL
ARo = No × EG × MG × IR
  • PA Page-to-Attendee Rate: attendees who signed up on the page ÷ page visits
  • RR registration rate: sign-ups ÷ page visits
  • SUf show-up rate of page sign-ups: page sign-ups who attended ÷ page sign-ups
  • ST stay rate: visits that stayed 10 seconds or more ÷ page visits (100% minus bounce rate)
  • FS form seen rate: people who scrolled to the form ÷ people who stayed
  • FT form start rate: people who started the form ÷ people who saw it
  • FC form finish rate: people who submitted ÷ people who started
  • SU0 show-up rate of page sign-ups who skipped the thank you page action
  • T1 thank you page action rate: people who did the action ÷ people who saw the thank you page
  • SU1 show-up rate of page sign-ups who did the thank you page action (add to calendar, join WhatsApp)
  • R registrants: page sign-ups plus auto-registrants
  • V page visits from every channel
  • AR auto-registrants: people registered without the form (calendar Yes or Maybe, interested replies, inbound messages)
  • ARc registered by a Yes or Maybe on the calendar invite
  • ARe registered by an interested reply to cold email
  • ARl registered by an interested reply on LinkedIn
  • ARo registered from inbound messages after posts
  • Nc calendar invites sent
  • DRc invite delivered rate: invites delivered ÷ invites sent
  • YS RSVP Yes rate: Yes ÷ invites delivered
  • MY RSVP Maybe rate: Maybe ÷ invites delivered
  • Ne people emailed
  • DRe email delivered rate: emails delivered ÷ emails sent
  • RPe email reply rate: people who replied ÷ people who received the email
  • YE email interested share: interested replies ÷ all email replies
  • Nl LinkedIn connection requests sent
  • ACl acceptance rate: requests accepted ÷ requests sent
  • MSl message reach: people messaged ÷ people who accepted
  • RPl LinkedIn reply rate: people who replied ÷ people messaged
  • YL LinkedIn interested share: interested replies ÷ all LinkedIn replies
  • No post impressions
  • EG post engagement rate: likes, comments and shares ÷ post impressions
  • MG inbound message rate: inbound messages ÷ engagements
  • IR inbound registered rate: inbound people registered ÷ inbound messages
Stage 3 Reminders and Nurture

Metric: SU (Show-Up Rate) = attendees ÷ registrants

SU = DJ + Je + Jw + Js + Jv
A = R × SU
Je = DRm × ORm × JCm
Jw = WO × DRw × RDw × JCw
Js = SRs × DRs × JCs
Jv = PH × AN × CF × CC
  • SU Show-Up Rate: attendees ÷ registrants
  • DJ direct join rate: people who joined without clicking any reminder ÷ registrants
  • Je joins from reminder email: people whose first join came from an email link ÷ registrants
  • Jw joins from WhatsApp: people whose first join came from a WhatsApp link ÷ registrants
  • Js joins from SMS: people whose first join came from a text link ÷ registrants
  • Jv joins after an AI call: people who joined after confirming on the call ÷ registrants
  • A attendees
  • R registrants: page sign-ups plus auto-registrants
  • DRm reminder email delivered rate: delivered ÷ sent
  • ORm reminder email open rate: people who opened a reminder ÷ people who got one
  • JCm email join click rate: people who joined from an email link ÷ people who opened
  • WO WhatsApp opt-in rate: people who opted in ÷ registrants
  • DRw WhatsApp delivered rate: delivered ÷ sent
  • RDw WhatsApp read rate: read ÷ delivered
  • JCw WhatsApp join click rate: people who joined from a WhatsApp link ÷ people who read
  • SRs SMS reach: registrants with a phone and text consent ÷ registrants
  • DRs SMS delivered rate: delivered ÷ sent
  • JCs SMS join click rate: people who joined from a text link ÷ texts delivered
  • PH phone share: registrants the AI caller can ring ÷ registrants
  • AN answer rate: calls answered by a person ÷ people called
  • CF confirm rate: people who said they will come ÷ calls answered
  • CC confirmed-and-came rate: people who joined ÷ people who confirmed
Stage 4 Booking the Call

Metric: BR (Booking Rate) = calls booked ÷ attendees

BR = BL + (1 − BL) × FU × BF + NS × RW × RC × BF
BL = SO × CT × BF
CT = EN × CE + (1 − EN) × CQ
NS = (R − A) ÷ A
B = A × BR
  • BR Booking Rate: calls booked ÷ attendees
  • BL live booking rate: calls booked during the event ÷ attendees
  • FU follow-up click rate: booking-link clicks from follow-up messages ÷ people who had not booked
  • BF booking finish rate: calls booked ÷ offer clicks
  • NS no-shows per attendee: (registrants − attendees) ÷ attendees
  • RW replay watch rate: replay viewers ÷ no-shows
  • RC replay click rate: offer clicks from the replay ÷ replay viewers
  • SO stayed for the offer: people present at the offer minute ÷ attendees
  • CT offer click rate: offer clicks ÷ people at the offer
  • EN engaged share: people who answered a poll, asked a question or chatted ÷ people at the offer
  • CE engaged click rate: offer clicks ÷ engaged people at the offer
  • CQ quiet click rate: offer clicks ÷ quiet people at the offer
  • R registrants: page sign-ups plus auto-registrants
  • A attendees
  • B calls booked
Stage 5 The Sales Pipeline

Metric: CL (Close Rate) = onboarded customers ÷ calls booked

CL = HD × QR × PR × WN × PY × OB
HD = SH + (1 − SH) × RB × SH
SH = SH0 + SHe + SHw + SHs + SHv
SHe = DRr × ORr × CSe
SHw = WP × DRw × RDw × CSw
SHs = SP × DRs × CSs
SHv = PV × AV × CSv
RB = F1 + F2 + F3 + F4 + F5
WN = W0 + DF1 + DF2 + DF3
C = B × CL
  • CL Close Rate: onboarded customers ÷ calls booked
  • HD held rate: calls held, including rebooked ones ÷ calls booked
  • QR qualified rate: qualified ÷ calls held
  • PR proposal rate: proposals sent ÷ qualified
  • WN win rate: proposals accepted ÷ proposals sent
  • PY payment rate: payments completed ÷ proposals accepted
  • OB onboarding rate: customers onboarded ÷ customers who paid
  • SH call show rate: calls held the first time ÷ calls booked
  • RB rebook rate: no-shows who rebooked ÷ no-shows (the five follow-ups added)
  • SH0 showed with no reminder click ÷ calls booked
  • SHe showed after confirming from the email reminder ÷ calls booked
  • SHw showed after confirming from the WhatsApp reminder ÷ calls booked
  • SHs showed after confirming from the SMS reminder ÷ calls booked
  • SHv showed after confirming on the AI call ÷ calls booked
  • DRr call reminder email delivered rate: delivered ÷ sent
  • ORr call reminder email open rate: opened ÷ delivered
  • CSe email confirm rate: confirmed and showed ÷ people who opened
  • WP WhatsApp reach for calls: booked contacts with WhatsApp ÷ calls booked
  • DRw WhatsApp delivered rate: delivered ÷ sent
  • RDw WhatsApp read rate: read ÷ delivered
  • CSw WhatsApp confirm rate: confirmed and showed ÷ read
  • SP SMS reach for calls: booked contacts with a phone and consent ÷ calls booked
  • DRs SMS delivered rate: delivered ÷ sent
  • CSs SMS confirm rate: confirmed and showed ÷ delivered
  • PV phone share for calls: booked contacts the AI caller can ring ÷ calls booked
  • AV confirmation call answer rate: answered ÷ called
  • CSv call confirm rate: confirmed and showed ÷ answered
  • F1 rebooked after no-show follow-up 1 (sent right away) ÷ no-shows
  • F2 rebooked after follow-up 2 (3 days later) ÷ no-shows
  • F3 rebooked after follow-up 3 (3 days after that) ÷ no-shows
  • F4 rebooked after follow-up 4 (3 days after that) ÷ no-shows
  • F5 rebooked after follow-up 5 (a week after that) ÷ no-shows
  • W0 accepted with no chasing ÷ proposals sent
  • DF1 accepted after deal follow-up 1 (day 3) ÷ proposals sent
  • DF2 accepted after deal follow-up 2 (day 7) ÷ proposals sent
  • DF3 accepted after deal follow-up 3 (day 12) ÷ proposals sent
  • C onboarded customers
  • B calls booked
Stage 6 The Next 52 Weeks

Metric: YB (Yearly Rebooking Rate) = calls booked from the 52-week messages ÷ people on the list

YB = Yn + Yi + Yw + Ys + Yv
Yn = RCn × SNn × DLn × OPn × CKn × BK
Yi = RCi × SNi × DLi × OPi × CKi × BK
Yw = RCw × SNw × DLw × OPw × CKw × BK
Ys = RCs × SNs × DLs × CKs × BK
Yv = RCv × SNv × ANv × BV
L = R − C
By = L × YB
  • YB Yearly Rebooking Rate: calls booked from the 52-week messages ÷ people on the list
  • Yn calls booked from weekly email ÷ people on the list (its share of the credit)
  • Yi calls booked from AI newsletter ÷ people on the list (its share of the credit)
  • Yw calls booked from WhatsApp ÷ people on the list (its share of the credit)
  • Ys calls booked from SMS ÷ people on the list (its share of the credit)
  • Yv calls booked from AI check-in calls ÷ people on the list
  • RCn weekly email reach: people who can get weekly email ÷ people on the list
  • SNn weekly email sends per person per year
  • DLn weekly email delivered rate: delivered ÷ sent
  • OPn weekly email open rate: opened ÷ delivered
  • CKn weekly email click rate: clicks ÷ messages opened
  • BK book-from-click rate: calls booked ÷ clicks from 52-week messages
  • RCi AI newsletter reach: people who can get AI newsletter ÷ people on the list
  • SNi AI newsletter sends per person per year
  • DLi AI newsletter delivered rate: delivered ÷ sent
  • OPi AI newsletter open rate: opened ÷ delivered
  • CKi AI newsletter click rate: clicks ÷ messages opened
  • RCw WhatsApp reach: people who can get WhatsApp ÷ people on the list
  • SNw WhatsApp sends per person per year
  • DLw WhatsApp delivered rate: delivered ÷ sent
  • OPw WhatsApp read rate: read ÷ delivered
  • CKw WhatsApp click rate: clicks ÷ messages read
  • RCs SMS reach: people who can get SMS ÷ people on the list
  • SNs SMS sends per person per year
  • DLs SMS delivered rate: delivered ÷ sent
  • CKs SMS click rate: clicks ÷ texts delivered
  • RCv AI call reach: people the AI caller can ring ÷ people on the list
  • SNv AI check-in calls per person per year
  • ANv check-in call answer rate: answered ÷ called
  • BV booked on the call: calls booked ÷ check-in calls answered
  • L people on the list: everyone known who did not become a customer
  • R registrants: page sign-ups plus auto-registrants
  • C onboarded customers
  • By calls booked from the 52 weeks
