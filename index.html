The One Equation to Memorize

The Funnel Equation
Revenue=Traffic × Capture × Show-Up × Booking × Close × Price, plus the 52 Weeks

Each word is a stage, and each stage is its own equation built from the numbers your tools already report: deliverability, opens, clicks, page speed, form starts, reminders read, minutes watched, polls answered, calls connected, proposals accepted. Multiply them in order and you get booked calls, customers and revenue, with nothing left to guess.

Booked calls is the north star. Everything to its left exists to produce it, and everything to its right turns it into revenue. The full equations, every KPI inside them and where each one is measured are in The Funnel Equations.

The three shapes share Stage 1, the pipeline and the 52 weeks. They differ in the middle: a webinar counts attendees, a reverse squeeze page counts people who watched more than half of the video, and a workshop series counts people who attended at least 1.5 of the three days.

The Rule That Runs Through the Whole Week

Never model your funnel with invented rates. Every symbol in these equations is a counted event with a named source. A rate is one count divided by another. Reference bands only tell you whether a number you measured is weak; they never stand in for one you have not measured yet.

How to Read These Equations

  1. Counts are capitals, rates are lower case. V is visits, R registrants, A attendees, B booked calls. A rate is always one count divided by the count before it.
  2. Rates in a row multiply. Registration is (1 − bounce) × form reach × form start × form completion. Fix the weakest factor and the product moves the most.
  3. Channels that chase the same person combine as a union. Show-up is 1 − (1 − base) × (1 − kemail)(1 − kwhatsapp)(1 − ksms)(1 − kvoice): someone comes if anything brings them, so each extra channel adds less.
  4. Split by entry path. Form registrants, calendar auto-registrants and lead form leads behave differently. A blended rate hides where the leak is.
  5. Health limits are not levers. Spam complaints, hard bounces, WhatsApp quality, SMS opt-outs and account restrictions are stop signs. Breach one and you pause, whatever the other numbers say.
  6. Reference bands judge, they never predict. Each band comes with its public source; where no credible source exists, it says so, and you set it from your first run.
Why Booked Calls Is the Number Everything Connects To

Every stage before it exists to produce a booked call; every stage after it turns calls into revenue. Ranking any KPI by how many booked calls it would add, if it reached the top of its band, is how the system decides what to fix next.

Summary: The Lessons in This Section
  1. Every stage and every channel has its own equation, and every KPI the tools report sits inside the one it moves.
  2. Stage 1 produces visits and auto-registrants; deliverability and page speed multiply every channel.
  3. Stage 2 and 3 count different things per shape: attendees for a webinar, viewers past 50% for a reverse squeeze page, people at 1.5 days or more for a workshop series.
  4. Reminder channels combine as a union; split every rate by entry path.
  5. Booked calls is the north star. The pipeline turns it into revenue one rate at a time, with no-show and deal follow-ups as recovery terms.
  6. The 52 weeks re-engage the pool every week and credit every booking back to its channel.
  7. The KPI with the largest ΔB is the element to fix. Health limits are stop signs, not levers.
